1. Purpose and scope

This page explains the privacy considerations for DriveSA, a proposed independent learning application for people preparing for driving licence knowledge tests in South Africa. The application is not operated or endorsed by any government, transport department or test authority.

2. Information that may be stored on a device

If enabled in the released application, local records can include practice progress, quiz and mock-test results, selected region, mistakes to review, settings and preferences. Whether a name or identifier is stored, and whether this information is synchronised to a server, must be confirmed against the released application.

3. Advertising and third-party providers

Where configured, an advertising SDK such as Google AdMob and its mediation partners may process device identifiers, approximate location derived from IP address, ad impressions or interactions, diagnostics and fraud-prevention signals. This is not a statement that advertising is already enabled in DriveSA. Final disclosure must list the SDKs that actually ship with the app and applicable privacy/consent choices.

4. Purchases and app stores

If the app offers Premium access or other in-app purchases, a provider such as RevenueCat and an app store may process purchase tokens, receipts, entitlements and transaction metadata. Verify the released integration, products, and refund/restore flows before finalising this section. Do not provide payment-card details by email.

5. Purposes of processing

Where applicable, information may be used to provide learning progress, fulfil purchases, run and measure ads, respond to support requests, diagnose technical problems and prevent misuse. Only purposes supported by the released app should remain in the final policy.

6. Data sharing and international processing

Third-party service providers, if integrated, may process data on infrastructure outside South Africa. The final policy must identify the relevant categories of recipients, international transfers and any required safeguards or consent arrangements.

7. Retention and deletion

Ordinary local data can generally be cleared through device storage settings or removal of the app, subject to device backups. Other information may be subject to provider, legal, security and accounting retention rules. See data deletion instructions for contact details. Actual retention periods must be verified.

8. Children and age requirements

Before release, confirm the application's intended age group and whether any child-directed advertising, parental-consent or other safeguards are required.

9. Privacy choices and questions

For privacy enquiries or requests about data under the developer's control, email smartmonetaryinvestments@gmail.com and identify DriveSA. Where applicable, device settings and third-party provider settings may offer additional privacy controls.

10. Updates

This template will be updated before release to reflect verified app behaviour and applicable legal requirements. Any final policy should display its actual effective date.
