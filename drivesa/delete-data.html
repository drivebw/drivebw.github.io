Request data deletion

For a request concerning information under the DriveSA developer's control, email smartmonetaryinvestments@gmail.com with the subject DriveSA Data Deletion Request. Describe the information you want removed, without sending payment-card details or passwords.

Remove local information

On Android, open Settings → Apps → DriveSA → Storage → Clear storage or Clear data, where supported. On iPhone, deleting the app usually removes ordinary app data. Some backed-up, securely stored or app-store records may remain under device or provider rules.

Third-party information

If advertising, analytics or payment services are enabled, their independently controlled records may require a separate provider request. The final deletion policy must accurately identify these services and the developer's retention and deletion procedures.

Account deletion

If DriveSA offers accounts, the final policy must describe the in-app and web account-deletion process required by applicable app-store rules. This draft does not assert that the app is account-free.
