1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use DriveBW. DriveBW is an independent driving licence learning and practice application for users preparing for Botswana driving licence tests.

DriveBW does not require you to create a DriveBW account or directly provide your name, phone number, postal address, or payment-card details. However, the app uses third-party advertising, consent-management, app-store, and purchase-validation services that may collect or process technical, advertising, purchase, and usage information as described below.

2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

DriveBW may store the following information locally on your device:

  • Learning progress, scores, quiz and mock-test history
  • App settings, theme preferences, onboarding status, and other preferences
  • Premium-entitlement cache and purchase-restoration status
  • Offline-usage allowances, cooldown dates, and ad-related frequency or session counters
  • Other information needed for the app to operate and remember your progress

Some information may be stored using secure device storage. Removing the app usually removes ordinary local app data, but certain secure-storage or app-store records may remain according to the operating system, device backup settings, or store-account rules.

3. Advertising and Google Mobile Ads

The free version of DriveBW displays advertising through Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Ads may include banner, interstitial, and rewarded formats.

Depending on your device, region, consent choices, and platform settings, Google and its advertising partners may process:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate a general location
  • Advertising identifiers and other device- or app-related identifiers
  • Advertising data, such as ads shown, impressions, and ad interactions
  • App and product interactions, such as app launches, taps, and video views
  • Device, operating-system, app-version, and network information
  • Crash logs, diagnostics, performance information, and fraud-prevention signals

Advertising may be personalized, non-personalized, limited, or technically served depending on applicable law, consent choices, account configuration, and device settings. Non-personalized ads may still use limited identifiers or local storage for functions such as frequency capping, aggregated reporting, security, and fraud prevention.

4. Consent and Privacy Choices

DriveBW uses Google's User Messaging Platform where required to request, store, and apply privacy choices relating to advertising and local device storage. Depending on your location and the privacy messages available for your device, you may be shown options concerning personalized ads, non-personalized ads, limited ads, or other data uses.

You may also control advertising identifiers and tracking permissions through your Android, Google, Apple, or iOS device settings. Choices available to you vary by device, operating system, account, and country.

5. Purchases, Premium Access, and RevenueCat

DriveBW uses RevenueCat to validate purchases, restore purchases, and determine whether the Premium entitlement is active. RevenueCat and the relevant app store may process information such as:

  • An anonymous or pseudonymous app-user identifier
  • Device type, operating system, app version, and technical information
  • Product identifiers, purchase dates, entitlement status, and subscription status
  • Apple receipt information or Google Play purchase tokens
  • Country, currency, store, and other transaction-related metadata
  • Information needed to detect fraud, validate purchases, and provide customer support

Payment-card details are processed by Apple App Store or Google Play and are not directly received or stored by DriveBW.
