Privacy Policy

App Name: DriveBW

Effective Date: 18th May 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how DriveBW operates and how your information is handled when you use the app.

Who This App Is For

DriveBW is a driving licence practice and learning application designed for users preparing for Botswana driving licence tests.

Information We Collect

DriveBW does not directly collect, store, or share personal information. The app operates primarily offline and does not require users to create an account. We do not use analytics tools, advertising trackers, or third-party tracking services.

How We Use Information

  • To provide driving licence practice and learning features
  • To locally store app settings and progress on your device
  • To verify premium subscription access through Google Play Store or Apple App Store services
  • To maintain and improve app functionality

Offline Functionality

Most DriveBW features operate offline using content stored directly on your device.

Subscription and Billing

Premium subscriptions and purchases are processed securely through Google Play Store and Apple App Store services. DriveBW does not directly store or process payment card information.

How Your Data Is Stored

DriveBW stores app settings and progress locally on your device. We do not store personal information on external servers.

Data Sharing

DriveBW does not sell, share, or distribute personal information because the app does not directly collect personal information.

Data Retention

DriveBW does not retain personal information on external servers. Any locally stored app data remains on your device unless removed by uninstalling the app.

Your Rights

  • You may cancel your subscription at any time through Google Play Store or Apple App Store settings
  • You may remove locally stored app data by uninstalling the app

Security

We take reasonable measures to ensure the app functions securely. However, no software or electronic storage method can guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

DriveBW does not knowingly collect personal information from children or users of any age.

International Use

DriveBW is intended for Botswana driving licence learning use. If you access the app from another location, you do so at your own discretion.

Third-Party Services

DriveBW may rely on platform services such as Google Play Store and Apple App Store for subscription processing, app distribution, and related platform functionality.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the app after changes are made means you accept the revised policy.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact smartmonetaryinvestments@gmail.com.

Governing Law
