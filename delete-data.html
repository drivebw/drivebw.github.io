Request deletion of your data

DriveBW does not require users to create a DriveBW account. Most learning progress, quiz results, mock-test history, settings and preferences are stored locally on your device.

To request deletion of data associated with DriveBW that is controlled by DriveBW, email:

smartmonetaryinvestments@gmail.com

Use the subject: DriveBW Data Deletion Request

If your request concerns a purchase, you may include your Google Play order or transaction reference so that we can identify the relevant purchase record. Do not send passwords or payment-card information.

Delete data stored on your device

You can remove locally stored DriveBW data using your Android device settings:

  1. Open Android Settings.
  2. Open Apps.
  3. Select DriveBW.
  4. Open Storage.
  5. Select Clear storage or Clear data.

Uninstalling DriveBW will also normally remove ordinary local app data.

Data that may be deleted

  • Information voluntarily provided to DriveBW support
  • Anonymous or pseudonymous identifiers under DriveBW's control
  • Purchase-entitlement information where deletion is technically and legally permitted
  • Other information directly controlled by DriveBW

Data that may be retained

Certain information may be retained when reasonably necessary for legitimate purposes such as:

  • Financial or accounting requirements
  • Purchase verification and refund handling
  • Fraud prevention and security
  • Legal or regulatory compliance
  • App-store requirements

Information retained for these purposes will only be kept for as long as reasonably necessary or required by applicable law.

Third-party services

DriveBW uses services including Google AdMob, Google Play, RevenueCat and Apple App Store services.

Some information is processed independently by these providers. DriveBW cannot directly delete information controlled solely by Google, Apple, RevenueCat or another third-party provider. Requests relating to those records may need to be made directly to the relevant provider.
