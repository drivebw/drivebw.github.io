DriveBW

DriveBW is a modern Botswana driving licence practice app designed to help learners prepare smarter, faster, and more confidently for their theory tests.


Instead of relying only on traditional study methods, DriveBW transforms learning into an interactive experience through realistic mock tests, section-based quizzes, answer reviews, and structured study material designed specifically for Botswana licence learners.


Whether you are preparing for your first learner’s licence or revising before your test date, DriveBW helps users build confidence by practicing questions in a clean, organized, and mobile-friendly environment anytime and anywhere.


With instant feedback, progress-focused learning, and an easy-to-use interface inspired by modern educational apps, DriveBW aims to make studying more accessible, engaging, and effective for Botswana drivers.
